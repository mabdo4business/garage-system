Design principles
Five rules decide every screen
1
Evidence before work
Nothing starts without a recorded approval. Photos, findings and approvals are one tap away on every repair order.
2
Fast at the counter
One search box finds any car by plate, mobile, name or VIN. Target: common tasks in three steps or fewer.
3
Built for the workshop floor
Tablet-first for technicians: large targets, high contrast, usable with gloves. No prices on their screens.
4
Arabic and English as equals
Every screen and document is designed in both directions from day one — never translated afterwards.
5
The safe action is the obvious one
Money, approvals and stock follow strict rules. When the system blocks something, it says why and what to do next.

Bilingual rules
Arabic and English, built in from the first screen
The layout mirrors
Menus, columns and arrows flip. Code uses logical CSS (start/end), never left/right.
Numbers stay left-to-right
Plates, phones, VINs and amounts are isolated so they never scramble inside Arabic.
One digit style
Western digits in both languages until the owners decide (D-28). A single setting switches.
Money and time
“AED 1,234.50” / “1,234.50 د.إ”, always two decimals. Dates and times in Asia/Dubai.
Bilingual documents
Invoices, credit notes and release notes carry both languages; wording per tax agent and lawyer.
Each person’s language
Staff pick theirs; customers receive links and messages in the language on their record.

13 · Text and chip colours meet 4.5:1 contrast on their backgrounds
Trust patterns
Six patterns that make the rules visible
Evidence on screen
Who approved, how, when and which version — one click from every job.
Correct, never delete
Credit notes and reversals appear in the history next to what they correct.
Blocked buttons say why
“Deliver — balance due 288.00” beats a grey button with no reason.
A second person for money out
Refunds, large discounts and admin changes need someone else, on their own PIN.
Pending is shown, not hidden
Waiting jobs and frozen lines stay visible with the reason in plain words.
Stale screens refuse politely
“This changed — reload” instead of silently overwriting someone else’s work.

Design targets we will test against

4.5 : 1
Minimum text contrast, in light and dark themes
18 px

≤ 3

Connection drops
A clear offline banner and printed fallback forms; no hidden offline mode in the first release.
Shared tablets
Quick user switch, idle lock, approvals always ask for the approver’s own PIN.
Screen readers
Every control labelled in both languages; status never shown by colour alone.
